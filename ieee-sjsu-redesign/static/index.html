IEEE SJSU

Engineering happens here.

The IEEE Student Branch at San José State University brings together some of Silicon Valley’s most promising emerging engineers, technologists, and innovators from across the region.

Through technical projects, professional development, and industry engagement, IEEE empowers students to develop the skills and experiences needed to succeed in a rapidly evolving technology landscape. Our members represent the next generation of talent driving Silicon Valley and beyond.

We run hands-on workshops covering PCB design, embedded systems, and digital logic, host speaker sessions and networking nights, and build real hardware projects. Membership is open to SJSU students across majors.
